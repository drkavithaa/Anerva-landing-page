The pattern beneath it

The pattern

High capability does not always produce internal stability.

Performance is rarely the first thing to fail. Recovery is.

Many capable people do not lack knowledge, ambition or intelligence. Their difficulty is that their internal capacity becomes unreliable under sustained demand.

The output still arrives. What changes is the price of producing it.

You may be praised for the very adaptation that is becoming unsustainable.

ANERVA does not read this as a character defect or a motivation problem. It asks a different question.

“How is this person’s brain–body system currently organised around demand, and what capacities must be trained for a more stable and adaptive response?”

You are not broken. You are patterned. This is never used to deny illness, trauma, disability, structural adversity or a person’s need for medical or psychological care.

The framework

Why you came. How your system is running.

A client category describes the primary human problem that brings a person into ANERVA. An operating mode describes the temporary organisation through which the brain–body system is responding to demand.

Neither is a diagnosis, identity, personality type, or protocol. ANERVA uses a proprietary, non-diagnostic category and Operating Mode framework.

Eight operating modes

A mode is a contextual configuration, not a person’s essence. It is time-bound, attached to a context, and it can change within a week. These eight are a set, not a scale — there is no first and no last.

Four separate questions

Category, mode, layer and phase are independent decisions. None is derived from another. Turn any dial and watch the other three hold.

Formulation

2,072 distinct entry formulations — seven entry categories, up to two ranked secondaries, and eight operating modes. Add context, resource and recovery modifiers and the framework distinguishes 157,472.

Capacity, not validity. This is how many distinct formulations ANERVA can express. It is not evidence that the framework is validated, and it is not a count of human types.

The method

The system proposes. A human assigns.

Scoring is arithmetic, never a model. No language model produces a score, a rank or a category. That boundary is permanent.

Where AI is, and is not.

AI never assigns a category or mode, never scores, never decides escalation, never publishes anything to a client. It drafts narrative from a confirmed assignment, and assists with synthesis, retrieval and language review. Every draft is held unpublished until a human reviews it.

Safety rules are arithmetic too. A raised flag stops the process and routes to a person, with no model involved in that decision, ever.

Data informs; it does not define. No score, category, biomarker, device or model can fully represent a human being.

The pathways

Two depths. One architecture.

Category count, symptom intensity and external status do not select the pathway. The work does.

Six weeks, nominal

ANA Core

Foundational architecture. Mapping the pattern, stabilising the system, interrupting the old response, and beginning to install a new one.

Twelve weeks, nominal

ANA Premium

Continues only where the earlier work reveals a clear, in-scope architecture problem requiring deeper installation, complexity integration or identity-level stabilisation.

Every week, either pathway

What a week contains

Weekly protocols, set by the architect after the session. Daily check-ins, under a minute each. A weekly review that decides what changes. Re-measurement at weeks 6 and 12.

The phase ladder

Every client enters at Phase 0. Phases advance on the architect’s explicit decision after a session — never on a calendar.

Six and twelve weeks are nominal structures, not a schedule and not a promise of change within that time.

Change timelines vary widely between individuals, which is why we measure at week 0 and again at weeks 6 and 12.

If Core does the work and deeper work is unnecessary, Core is where it ends. Premium is never manufactured as the expected next sale.

What we know, and what we do not

We would rather be exact than impressive.

ANERVA states what it knows, what it infers, what it is testing and what it does not yet know. This section is the last of those.

Reliability, not accuracy.

The scoring sheet applies the same rules to the same answers every time. That is reliability. Accuracy is a different claim, and it requires test–retest, inter-rater agreement and convergent validity. None of that exists yet, so we do not claim it.

No outcome dataset yet.

Week-0, week-6 and week-12 measurement is being put in place now. Until it has run, there are no outcome figures to publish, and we will not manufacture any.

Proprietary, not validated.

The seven categories and eight modes are proprietary ANERVA constructs. Their practical usefulness does not permit us to present them as validated scientific types.

A defined path, not a promise.

Validation has a sequence: content validity, cognitive interviews, inter-rater reliability, context sensitivity, construct validity, incremental utility, fairness and safety, then prospective validation. Predictive value, null findings and limits will all be reported.

Scope

ANERVA is developmental and performance-oriented. It is not psychotherapy, psychiatry, counselling or a mental-health crisis service. It is not medical diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a substitute for a licensed clinician. It is not a personality test that permanently categorises a person.

ANERVA may support people who experience stress, overwhelm, fatigue, reactivity or impaired focus. Its role in that is training and developmental support, not treatment of those conditions.

Adults only. Exclusion is not a judgement of worth. Referral is an act of care.

ANERVA is not a crisis service and is not monitored live. If you are at immediate risk, or something needs urgent medical or psychiatric attention, contact your local emergency services or a qualified clinician.
